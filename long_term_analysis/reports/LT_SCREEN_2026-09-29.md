- **Operating companies — FCFF DCF (Koller)**: 10-year explicit period from TTM revenue; year-1 growth = consensus next-year revenue growth, fading linearly to terminal g + 1pp; operating margin = TTM (cyclicals: average over the available fiscal years); reinvestment = growth ÷ RONIC, with RONIC = ROIC **ex-goodwill** (capped at 20% where capex > 25% of revenue — returns on an AI capex surge are unproven); Key-Value-Driver terminal value. Scenarios per Framework 4: Bear = 0.7× growth, −300bp margin, terminal RONIC = WACC − 1%; Base; Bull = 1.3× growth, +200bp, terminal RONIC up to 20%. Net cash = cash + ST investments − total debt (incl. leases where yfinance includes them).
- **Banks, insurers, balance-sheet fintechs, BRK-B — excess-return model**: book value + PV of (ROE − cost of equity) × book, ROE fading over 10 years (Bear: −3pp and spread vanishes; Base: half the spread persists; Bull: +2pp, ¾ persists). Normalised ROE = 3-year average + TTM, unusual items stripped for banks/insurers. BRK-B uses a 10% operating ROE (GAAP ROE includes mark-to-market gains) [assumption].
- **Regulated utilities**: same excess-return model, ROE capped at 12% (allowed-return territory). UK water (SVT, UU) is valued on RCV, which is not in the data → n/m.
- **Alternative asset managers (BX, KKR, ARES, ICG)**: equity Key-Value-Driver on consensus forward EPS (distributable-earnings basis). GAAP consolidation (funds, carry, non-controlling units) makes book/GAAP ROE non-representative — flagged.
- **REITs**: US: Gordon growth on AFFO (FFO ≈ NI + D&A − unusual items; maintenance haircut 2–15%) at g 1.0/2.5/3.5%. UK/EU IFRS REITs: same on a recurring-earnings proxy (operating income − interest). BBOX and VNA → n/m (reported operating income includes revaluation losses).
- **MoS Gate** (Framework 4): BEAR = price ≤ Bear value; BASE = price ≤ Base; NONE (< Bull) = between Base and Bull; **NONE (> Bull) = priced for perfection**.
- **Compounder** (Framework 8): 9 criteria. For balance-sheet businesses, FCF conversion and capex intensity are n/a and ROIC is replaced by ROE, so the 7+/9 and 4–6/9 cut-offs are applied proportionally (≥ 78% of scorable = COMPOUNDER; 44–77% = QUALITY). Management ownership uses aggregate insider % (founder vs professional CEO not distinguished). Buyback discipline passes only if the share count fell *and* the price is at or below Base value today. The business verdict is then gated by MoS: COMPOUNDER needs BEAR, QUALITY needs BASE, otherwise WATCH.
- **F-Score** (Scoring Model): Value (price/Base DCF 60%, forward P/E or P/FFO 40%), Quality (ROIC ex-goodwill level, operating margin, 3-yr FCF/NI, accrual ratio, D/E; financials: normalised ROE and its stability), Growth (3-yr revenue CAGR, consensus next-year growth, 3-yr EPS CAGR), weighted by Track (A 40/40/20, B 20/40/40, C 15/35/50). **Undervalued\*** = the score clears 62 but the price is above the Bull DCF — the score is overridden by the only price-anchored input.

## Method caveats

- This is a mechanical, same-rules screen across 167 very different businesses. Its job is ranking and filtering, not a final price target: a Deep-Dive re-derives margins, growth and WACC per company from filings and Recent Context.
- yfinance statements are not filing-verified; segment data, RPO/backlog, NRR, NIM, CET1, combined ratios, EPRA NTA and RCV are **not** in the data, so the Sector Appendix metrics are only partly represented here (the Deep-Dive's step 10 covers them).
- Forward growth uses yfinance consensus next-year revenue growth; where analysts are few (EU mid-caps) that input is noisy.
- High discount rates (US 5.27% rf) dominate: at a ~9% WACC, many NONE (< Bull) names move into BASE. The ±1pp column shows which.
- No position sizing and no stop levels (inviolable rules): thesis invalidation is the only exit trigger this agent deals in.
