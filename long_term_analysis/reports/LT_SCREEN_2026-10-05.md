## Method caveats

- This is a mechanical, same-rules screen across 167 very different businesses. Its job is ranking and filtering, not a final price target: a Deep-Dive re-derives margins, growth and WACC per company from filings and Recent Context.
- yfinance statements are not filing-verified; segment data, RPO/backlog, NRR, NIM, CET1, combined ratios, EPRA NTA and RCV are **not** in the data, so the Sector Appendix metrics are only partly represented here (the Deep-Dive's step 10 covers them).
- Forward growth uses yfinance consensus next-year revenue growth; where analysts are few (EU mid-caps) that input is noisy.
- High discount rates (US 5.24% rf) dominate: at a ~9% WACC, many NONE (< Bull) names move into BASE. The ±1pp column shows which.
- No position sizing and no stop levels (inviolable rules): thesis invalidation is the only exit trigger this agent deals in.
