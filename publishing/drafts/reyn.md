The shares trade at USD 21.85, a trailing P/E of 13.4x and a forward P/E of
13.0x on consensus forward earnings of USD 1.68 — in the middle of a
52-week range of USD 20.44 to 27.32.

The case for the rating. Return on equity of 14.9% and a PEG of 0.64
alongside positive free cash flow describe a business priced in line with,
not below, its growth. The forward multiple already sits close to the
trailing one, which is consistent with the platform's own Fair rating
(F-score 53.3) rather than a name mispriced in either direction.

What the current multiple is pricing. Revenue growth of 0.6% and earnings
growth concentrated in cost and mix rather than volume describe a mature
packaging business, not one set up for multiple expansion. At a forward
P/E already near 13x, the case for holding rests less on re-rating and more
on the dividend yield of approximately 4.2% carrying most of the expected
total return over the horizon.

Thesis invalidation. A move in the forward P/E materially below its current
13.0x — without a corresponding move in forward earnings — would signal the
market pricing a volume or margin deterioration beyond what the current
figures show, at which point the flat-multiple assumption behind this
rating would need restating.
