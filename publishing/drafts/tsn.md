The shares trade at USD 51.42, a trailing P/E of 31.7x that collapses to a
forward P/E of 12.1x on consensus forward earnings of USD 4.27 — the gap
between the two multiples is the clearest fact on the page and reflects a
market already pricing a large step-up in near-term earnings.

The case for the rating. Q3 2026 operating income rose 39% year-on-year on
flat sales, and management raised full-year revenue growth guidance to
2.5-3.5%. A PEG of 0.16 says the forward multiple has not caught up with
that earnings trajectory, which argues against the stock being expensive
at current levels.

What the current multiple is pricing. The company itself frames the
quarter as chicken segment strength offsetting a beef segment under
pressure from structural livestock constraints — the forward EPS consensus
already assumes that offset continues to hold. Trailing ROE of 4.0% is
low relative to the forward earnings implied by the 12.1x multiple,
meaning the market is pricing a margin recovery that has not yet shown up
in trailing results.

Thesis invalidation. A beef-segment deterioration that is not offset by
chicken-segment strength — visible as a miss against the 2.5-3.5% FY26
revenue growth guidance, or a walk-back of that guidance itself — is the
point at which the forward earnings consensus behind this rating would
need restating.
