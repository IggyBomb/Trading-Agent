The shares trade at USD 74.38, a trailing P/E of 48.6x that falls to 16.4x
on consensus forward earnings of USD 4.53 — a gap that reflects an
expected earnings recovery from currently depressed trailing profitability
(ROE of 8.7%).

The case for the rating. Even after Wells Fargo cut its price target to
USD 95 from USD 110 on 3 September, that reduced target still implies
27.7% upside from the current price — comfortably above the threshold this
rating requires. A PEG of 1.33 on the forward multiple is not demanding
for a name priced for a cyclical recovery in power and sensing demand.

What the discount to that target is pricing. The announced all-stock
acquisition of Synaptics — approximately USD 7 billion, 1.35 ON shares per
Synaptics share, targeted to close mid-2027 — is a real integration and
share-count overhang: the deal is dilutive on a per-share basis until the
combined entity's earnings catch up, and remains subject to shareholder
and regulatory approval over a roughly nine-to-twelve month runway. The
Wells Fargo cut itself, coming after the deal was announced, is consistent
with the market discounting near-term execution risk rather than the
underlying semiconductor cycle.

Thesis invalidation. A deterioration in the terms or timeline of the
Synaptics acquisition — a revised exchange ratio, a regulatory objection,
or a stated delay past mid-2027 — is the level at which this thesis would
need restating. The 16 September Analyst Day is the nearest scheduled point
at which management commentary on integration progress would first surface.
