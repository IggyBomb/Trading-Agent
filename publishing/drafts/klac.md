The shares trade at USD 185.60 following a 7.3% move on the Q4 FY2026
print, a trailing P/E of 50.7x that falls to 27.75x on consensus forward
earnings of USD 6.69 — already one of the richer multiples in the
semiconductor equipment group.

The case for the rating. Q4 revenue of USD 3.66 billion beat the USD 3.61
billion consensus, full-year revenue reached USD 13.58 billion, and return
on equity of 76.1% is exceptional even against the sector. Sell-side
consensus places the 12-month target at USD 233.77 across 29 analysts,
implying 25.9% upside and a re-rating of the forward multiple from 27.75x
toward roughly 35x.

What the premium is pricing. A PEG of 3.67 says the market is not paying
for earnings growth on a like-for-like basis — it is paying for KLA's
position in process control as AI-driven wafer fab equipment spending
continues. That is not universally shared: UBS cut its target to USD 200
from USD 215 in mid-August, well below the USD 233.77 consensus average,
and closer to the current price than the average analyst view. The
dispersion between USD 200 and USD 233.77 is itself a statement about how
much of the AI capex cycle is already in the price.

Thesis invalidation. A semiconductor capital expenditure slowdown —
visible first in wafer fab equipment order commentary from KLA's largest
customers — is the point at which the forward multiple would need to
compress back toward the lower end of the current analyst range, not the
consensus target this rating is built on.
