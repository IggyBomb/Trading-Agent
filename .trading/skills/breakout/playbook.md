# Skill: Breakout

## When to Load
Load this skill when the user asks to analyze, find, or trade breakout setups.

## Setup Definition
A breakout occurs when price closes above a well-defined resistance level (or below support for shorts) on expanding volume, signaling a potential trend continuation or new trend initiation.

## Fundamental Gate — Non-Negotiable
Before any technical analysis is performed, the following must all be true. If any fail, stop — do not proceed to technicals.

- **Fundamentals are healthy**: revenue growth, positive or improving earnings, manageable debt, strong or improving margins
- **Financials pass basic checks**: no red flags in balance sheet (e.g. cash burn, dilution risk, upcoming debt maturity)
- **Narrative is positive**: recent news, sector trend, and macro context support the bull thesis — not working against it
- **No major near-term risk events** that could invalidate the setup (earnings surprise risk, regulatory overhang, etc.)

Only if all four pass do you continue to the technical analysis below.

---

## Entry Criteria
- Price must close above resistance (not just wick through)
- Volume on breakout candle must be > 1.5x the 20-day average
- Breakout level must have been tested at least twice before
- Prefer breakouts in the direction of the broader market trend
- Ideal entry: retest of broken level as new support (confirmation entry)

## Stop-Loss Placement
- Stop below the breakout level (or last swing low)
- Never more than 2% of account at risk
- If using confirmation entry: stop below the retest candle low

## Targets
- Minimum R:R: 1.5:1
- Target 1: measured move (height of the base added to breakout point)
- Target 2: next major resistance level
- Scale out 50% at Target 1, trail stop on remainder

## Conviction Filters
- High: clean level, volume surge, trend aligned, clear measured move
- Medium: decent level, moderate volume, trend neutral
- Low: skip — do not trade

## Output Format
TICKER | Breakout | Entry Zone | Stop | Target | Conviction
