# Skill: Pullback

## When to Load
Load this skill when the user asks to analyze, find, or trade pullback setups.

## Setup Definition
A pullback occurs when price retraces to a key level (moving average, prior resistance turned support, or Fibonacci level) within an established trend, offering a lower-risk entry in the direction of the trend.

## Fundamental Gate — Non-Negotiable
Before any technical analysis is performed, the following must all be true. If any fail, stop — do not proceed to technicals.

- **Fundamentals are healthy**: revenue growth, positive or improving earnings, manageable debt, strong or improving margins
- **Financials pass basic checks**: no red flags in balance sheet (e.g. cash burn, dilution risk, upcoming debt maturity)
- **Narrative is positive**: recent news, sector trend, and macro context support the bull thesis — not working against it
- **No major near-term risk events** that could invalidate the setup (earnings surprise risk, regulatory overhang, etc.)

Only if all four pass do you continue to the technical analysis below.

---

## Entry Criteria
- Primary trend must be clearly established (series of higher highs/lows for longs)
- Pullback should be orderly — decreasing volume on retracement is ideal
- Entry triggers:
  - Bounce off 20 EMA or 50 SMA in a strong trend
  - Return to prior breakout level (support retest)
  - Fibonacci 38.2%–61.8% retracement of the last impulse leg
- Wait for a reversal candle (hammer, engulfing, inside bar) at the level before entering

## Stop-Loss Placement
- Stop below the pullback low (or below the key level being tested)
- If using EMA bounce: stop 1 ATR below the EMA
- Never more than 2% of account at risk

## Targets
- Minimum R:R: 1.5:1
- Target 1: prior swing high (for longs)
- Target 2: measured move continuation
- Scale out 50% at Target 1, trail stop on remainder

## Conviction Filters
- High: strong trend, low-volume pullback to key level, clear reversal candle, market aligned
- Medium: trend present, pullback to level but candle not ideal or volume mixed
- Low: skip — choppy trend or no clear level

## Output Format
TICKER | Pullback | Entry Zone | Stop | Target | Conviction
