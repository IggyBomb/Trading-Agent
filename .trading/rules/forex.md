# Forex-Specific Rules

These rules apply ONLY to FX pairs (contain a slash, e.g. EUR/USD).

## Session awareness
- London session: 08:00-16:00 GMT (highest liquidity)
- New York session: 13:00-21:00 GMT (second highest)
- Avoid entries in Asian session for majors

## Sizing
- Always calculate in pips, not points
- Standard lot = 100,000 units; micro lot = 1,000 units
- Factor in spread cost (typically 0.5-2 pips for majors)

## Pairs to avoid
- Exotics with spread > 10 pips
- Any pair during central bank announcement windows
